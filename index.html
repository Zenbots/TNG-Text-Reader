Capture Photo takes a single still frame, runs OCR on it, and drops the result into the text box for you to review before reading. Live Scan keeps sampling the camera every few seconds and speaks new text aloud as it recognizes it — handy for a page, a sign, or a label, hands-free. It works best with clear, well-lit, reasonably large text held steady for a moment; small or blurry print may be missed.

Not sharing

Click Share Screen and choose a screen, window, or tab. Then drag a box over the preview above to outline the area you want read aloud — Start Reading Box samples just that area every few seconds and speaks new text as it recognizes it, so you can point it at a document, subtitles, or anything else and it keeps reading as the content changes. Draw a new box any time to move where it's watching. Read Whole Screen instead OCRs the entire shared frame once into the text box.
